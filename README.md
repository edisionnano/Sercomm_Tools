# Sercomm Tools
Tools for decrypting and rooting routers made by Sercomm. The repository focuses on routers provided by Greek ISPs with other routers covered as well due to similarities with them.

## Supported Models
➣ [SHG3060 & Easybox 805](https://github.com/edisionnano/SHG3060/) - Also known as the Greek Vodafone Power Station WiFi 6, the Greek Vox 3.0 and VD5244BV2

➣ [Speedport W 724V Type Ci](W724V) - Probably the first Sercomm router provided by a Greek ISP. This one is from 2013 and was provided by OTE (now Cosmote Telekom)

➣ [Three 4g+ Hub](LTE2122GR) - 4G+ router by UK ISP Three, software is actully made by Vodafone and is pretty close to the SHG3060 while the hardware is closer to the H300S. Also known as LTE2122GR.

➣ [Speedport Plus 2](VD4224BDT) - RDK-B router provided by Cosmote Telekom, also known as VD4224BDT and the black one

➣ [H300S & Speedport Plus](h300s_plus) - The Vodafone H300s and Speedport Plus are two very popular routers in the Greek market with a lot of similarities under the hood
